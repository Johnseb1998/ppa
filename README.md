# Healing Mind Platform

## Overview
Healing Mind is a comprehensive dual-interface Telehealth, Psychometric Testing, and Clinical Practice Management platform designed explicitly for psychologists, therapists, clinical administrators, and their clients.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Advanced Psychometric Assessment Portal
The platform serves as a powerful diagnostic and assessment engine for clinical use, including:
- **Automated Scoring & Normative Interpretation:** Built-in logic for industry-standard assessments (e.g., PHQ-9, AICS, Raven's SPM, YPI-V, BDI, BAI, ADHD scales).
- **Client-Facing Testing:** A secure, distraction-free interface for clients to take assessments digitally.
- **Therapist Analytics:** Instantaneous scoring, severity categorization, and historical tracking of test results to inform clinical SOAP notes and treatment plans.

### 2. Clinical Practice & Client Management
A robust suite of tools designed to streamline the workflow of therapists and clinics:
- **Unified Client Records:** Seamless registration flows capturing clinical consent, personal demographics, health history, and family context.
- **Therapist Dashboard:** A daily operational hub featuring a schedule grid, patient analytics, task assigner (for CBT homework), and secure clinical notes.
- **Admin & Oversight:** A specialized portal for clinic administrators to monitor therapist presence, track new client registrations, oversee clinical QA, and manage HR/operations.

### 3. Integrated Telehealth & Progress Tracking
- **Secure Communication:** Custom WebRTC video and chat integration for remote sessions.
- **Dynamic Task Assignment:** Therapists can assign daily psychoeducational tasks or CBT homework.
- **Smart Analytics:** The platform automatically calculates homework completion logic and tracks patient progress over time.

## Architecture & Security
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Strict separation of privileges between Clients, Therapists, and Admins.
- **Data Privacy:** Designed to handle sensitive health histories, familial context, and psychometric scores securely.
